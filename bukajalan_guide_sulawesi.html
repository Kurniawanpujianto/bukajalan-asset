About Sulawesi

Indonesia's most underrated island

Sulawesi is the island that changed how we see Indonesia. It is not the most visited — and that is exactly the point. While tourists crowd Bali and Lombok, Sulawesi quietly holds some of the most extraordinary experiences this country has to offer: ancient funeral ceremonies, untouched coral reefs, highland landscapes, and coastal communities that have barely changed in generations.

We have been travelling Sulawesi since 2019. We keep going back. This is our attempt to open the same road for you.
