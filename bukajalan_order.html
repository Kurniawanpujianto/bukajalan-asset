About this guide

Everything we know about Toraja

After three visits to Toraja spanning five years, we put everything we know into this guide. Not the usual tourist itinerary — but the real places, the honest logistics, and the cultural context that makes the difference between a good trip and an unforgettable one.

This is a digital PDF guide of 50+ pages. You receive it instantly after purchase, directly in your inbox. You can use it offline on your phone — ideal for areas with limited connectivity.

Who is this for?

First-timers and experienced travellers

Whether this is your first time in Indonesia or your tenth, Toraja rewards preparation. The guide works equally well for independent travellers and people planning with a local guide. It covers everything from the basics to the things no one else tells you.

Full guide contents

  • Getting to Toraja from Makassar — all transport options with current prices
  • Where to stay — from budget guesthouses to the best boutique options
  • The best local restaurants and where to find authentic Torajan food

  • Torajan coffee — where to find the best and how to buy it right
  • Local phrases in Torajan language
  • Practical info: SIM cards, money, safety, health
  • Day trips from Rantepao
