FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

What exactly is included in a Travel Guide?+

Our Travel Guides are detailed PDF documents with everything you need to know before and during your trip — recommended places to stay, where to eat, what to visit, local tips, transport advice, and a suggested itinerary. All based on our own experience.

Can I pay in Indonesian Rupiah?+

We are working on adding a Rupiah payment option via local bank transfer. If you need this already, please contact us directly and we will work something out.

How long does it take to get a custom itinerary?+

After receiving your request, we usually come back to you within 2–3 working days with a first proposal. More complex itineraries may take a little longer, but we will always keep you informed.

Do you speak Indonesian?+

Yes — Alex is Indonesian and we can communicate in Bahasa Indonesia, Dutch, and English. We are also working on adding a Bahasa Indonesia version of the website.

Can I change the language of the website?+

We are working on adding a Bahasa Indonesia option for the website. This is coming soon — stay tuned via our Instagram for updates.
