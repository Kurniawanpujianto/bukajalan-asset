Our Story

From the Netherlandsto Indonesia, and back

We are Maartje and Alex — a mixed couple from the Netherlands and Indonesia. From the moment we met, we made a promise to each other: to discover as much as possible of this beautiful country.

During our travels, we focus on finding Indonesia's authentic culture and untouched nature — the places beyond the usual tourist routes. By bundling all our experiences into our Travel Guides and customized Travel Itineraries, we hope to open the same road for you, so you can experience Indonesia the way we do.
